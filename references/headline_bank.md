# Headline / hook bank

Josh-supplied long-form headline and hook lines, for use alongside the
shorter punchy headlines already used across posters (e.g. "STUDY AND PAID
INTERNSHIP IN SINGAPORE"). These read more like a tagline or hook sentence
than a short 2-4 word headline line, so they usually work best as:

- the `tagline` field (most templates), or
- a `headline_lines` entry split across 2-3 shorter lines if the template's
  headline area can't fit a long sentence on one line, or
- the hook/subtext line on templates that have one (e.g. `night_glow`'s
  `session_description`, `magazine_split`'s subtitle-style fields).

**Important: use the line as-is, with no leading number** ("1.", "2.", etc.)
— those were only for listing them here.

**Per the skill rule in `SKILL.md`: whenever one of these is used as the
main header/hook, still make sure "Study + Paid Internship" (or clear
equivalent wording) is visible elsewhere on the poster** — since none of
these lines mention the program itself, only the destination/opportunity
angle. Add it as a short eyebrow, badge, or a second headline line.
